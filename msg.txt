test: cover english-page-content, the last shortcode with no fixture

The Foundation ships three shortcodes. `gallery` and `video` got fixtures long
ago; `english-page-content` never did, in the fixture or in the demo site, so
it had no coverage at all. It is a one-liner over `hugo.Sites`, which makes that
conspicuous: the component most likely to break on a Hugo deprecation is the one
nothing renders.

`fixtures/demo/content/shortcodes/english-content.md` pulls in `/work/alpha/`,
and the recorded snapshot shows what came back — the target page's body, ahead
of the fixture page's own text. The call is a real one; the output is not
nothing.

## What this does not cover

The fixture is single-language, so the English site the shortcode resolves *is*
the site and the call never crosses a language boundary. Asserted: the
`hugo.Sites` lookup, the `GetPage` path, and the `.Content` emission. A
deprecation of `hugo.Sites` — the exact thing §13.1 item 1 records as already
migrated — would now fail the gate.

Not asserted: the branch that matters most, a translation pulling the English
page's body. That needs a bilingual fixture, which is issue #6 and has been
outstanding long enough to be worth saying plainly rather than leaving to §13.2.

`llms.txt` gains the new page, which is the only other snapshot that moves.
