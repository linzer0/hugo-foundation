Foundation is now a site as well as a theme

The repository split into a theme at the root and a starter site at site/.

Published
September 29, 2026
Reading time
1 min read

The Foundation is no longer just a directory of partials you wire into your own site. Clone it and hugo server --source site gives you a working article site; add it as a submodule and you get the same View layer under someone else’s content.

The split is enforced by Hugo, not by discipline. A theme’s hugo.yaml is merged into every consuming build, and its content/ is mounted into the consuming site. The repository root holds only what a consumer should inherit; site material lives in site/. See docs/BASE-THEME.md for the verified mechanism.
