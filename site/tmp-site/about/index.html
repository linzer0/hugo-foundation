What this site is, and what to delete.

Reading time
1 min read

This is the Foundation’s own starter site. It exists so that cloning the repository gives you something running, instead of a directory of partials you have to assemble yourself.

Delete what you did not write. The two articles and the note are the worked example; the home page is a composition you will replace on day one. What stays useful is the machinery underneath: the archetypes, the --fn-* surface, and the content model.

Replace the placeholders in hugo.yaml — baseURL, title, description — and you have a site. Nothing in the neutral layer hardcodes a domain, so there is nothing else to hunt down.
